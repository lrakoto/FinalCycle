How it works

From questionnaire
to conversation.

    Share your revenue range, team size, client volume, and the tools you use to run the firm.

Before you begin.

Can I start if I’m still exploring?

Yes. You can fill out the questionnaire whether you have a transition in mind or are just starting to think about your options.

Do I need to know what my firm is worth?

The questionnaire asks for your own estimate. You can choose “Unsure” if you don’t have one yet.

What should I have handy?

Think about your firm’s annual revenue, team size, client count, and annual tax return volume. The form gives you ranges to choose from.
